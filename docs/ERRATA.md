# Known corrections and interpretation changes

This list preserves corrections rather than silently replacing historical claims. Read each stage's status and evidence notes for local details.

1. **A bounded loss is not a fixed loss scale.** A uniform upper bound survives any further positive shrinking and preserves relative hierarchy prices. The fixed-amplitude pilot instead imposes maximum outcome-wise loss oscillation exactly one on the complete report domain. Earlier wording that called a mere bounded-loss normalization a fixed scale must be read with this correction. The degree-price theorems are not withdrawn.
2. **Phase 9 auxiliary asymmetry provenance is narrower than a final-manuscript rerun.** The historical source hash belongs to the research proof copy, not the final manuscript appendix; mathematical content agrees but explanatory/citation text differs. A dedicated historical stdout log was not located. Preserved values and source correspondence are evidence of consistency, not a newly authenticated historical execution.
3. **Earlier open questions have different later status.** Phase 1's four-state source-mass order gap is sharpened in Phase 4. Phase 8's arbitrary-weight four-state degree-order gap is closed by Phase 9. Phase 10 covers the last two capacities, not all capacities. The earlier audit's proposed additive-risk screen is subsequently completed within the pilot's limited scope.
4. **The Phase 3 lower-bound interface is restricted.** The signed nonconstant construction replaces an earlier weaker constant-target construction. Its supplied modules/signs and fresh paired-loss-only evidence interface must not be omitted when stating the lower bound.
5. **Phase 6 endpoint and accounting limits remain.** Passing the prespecified mean endpoint does not show per-seed dominance, smallest-budget superiority or minimum-cost improvement. H128 and R138 differ by 32 retained bytes and are approximately matched. Initial smoke-run corrections are retained; local prospective records are not external registration.
6. **The pilot's execution evidence is local.** Source-level separation of reference truth and inference is not process-level blinding. Local hashes, timestamps and an exclusive lock support the recorded single attempt; they do not certify every unrecorded external action. The protocol predates confirmation labels but not all analytic work, and its earliest hash did not bind the later complete inference implementation.
7. **Publication hashes identify public derivatives.** Sanitized paths, metadata and reproduction wrappers can change source hashes. Where this occurs, publication provenance separates original identities from derivative identities and leaves scientific values unchanged. A refreshed publication hash does not authenticate an earlier run.

These qualifications do not turn finite arithmetic checks into proofs of continuous-domain claims, do not turn one simulated result into an empirical power estimate, and do not establish historical priority or external peer review.
