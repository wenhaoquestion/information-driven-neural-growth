# Public research archive: scope and provenance

This archive brings together Phases 1–10 and the subsequent fixed-amplitude additive-risk pilot. It preserves distinct research questions, historical negative findings and later refinements. It is not a single unified submission, a continuously running system, or evidence that every early claim generalizes to an ordinary neural network.

The source research archive remains separate and unchanged. Public copies remove private execution metadata, host-specific paths, collaboration instructions, service identifiers and unrelated files. Some author-facing reviews are presented as neutral technical notes. Publication transforms and their scope are recorded alongside the affected materials; changes to paths or metadata do not constitute a new experiment.

Where a public source hash differs from a historical source hash, a publication manifest attests only the consistency of the published derivative. It does not retroactively authenticate the original execution. Exact scientific values, frozen statistical counts, relevant uncertainty and recorded negative findings are retained.

## What the archive supports

- Finite-representation hierarchy results, with explicit source, loss, capacity and weight assumptions.
- Controlled synthetic neural-growth studies, including fresh-evidence costs, retained-data experiments, adverse controls and limits of the measured advantages.
- Exact and approximate memory/query results in their stated mathematical models.
- A four-state, known-mass, fixed-special-loss additive-risk benchmark with deterministic scale bounds and a single preselected statistical confirmation.

The later hierarchy papers do not replace the earlier learning studies. Phase 10 solves the last two capacities, not every prescribed capacity or every hierarchy budget. The statistical pilot estimates a population optimal-refit hierarchy penalty, not the performance of a trained growth policy.

Internal technical review and reproducible arithmetic are not external peer review, author sign-off, formal proof-assistant verification, institutional endorsement or exhaustive priority certification. Public release does not change the working-paper status.

## Excluded categories

- Private conversations, collaboration prompts, internal handoffs and service/storage identities.
- Credentials, local environments, caches, temporary builds and machine-specific execution logs.
- Downloaded third-party papers and figures whose redistribution permission was not established. Bibliographic references and source links remain.
- Duplicated replay trees and bundled copies of earlier releases.
- Large generated training/evaluation arrays and redundant per-trial output. Stage-specific manifests identify the excluded scientific artifacts, their roles and regeneration instructions.

Compact core records needed to independently audit published results may be included losslessly compressed. Their restoration procedure and original/uncompressed hashes are explicit. They are not an attempt to upload the entire multi-gigabyte local archive.

See [exclusion and recovery guide](EXCLUDED_ARTIFACTS.md), [reproduction guide](REPRODUCING.md), and [publication validation](VALIDATION.md). A missing raw array means the corresponding full saved-state audit requires regeneration; a summary-only check must not be described as a full rerun.
