# Original research illustration

`representation-growth-schematic.png` and `.svg` are the project's original Phase 1 illustration of incompatible flat optima at two and three representation states. The panels are a **schematic**, not empirical training results, neural architecture diagrams or a quantitative plot. The small reassignment arrow illustrates the construction discussed in the finite-representation manuscripts.

Author: Wenyu Huang, University of California San Diego. Scientific source: `phases/phase1/code/plot_results.py`; the corresponding original figure is also retained with Phase 1. No third-party figure was incorporated.

Suggested website caption: **Information-driven neural growth — theory, controlled experiments and statistical limits of irreversible representation growth.**

The repository has no designated open-source license. Inclusion here does not supply a separate image license.
