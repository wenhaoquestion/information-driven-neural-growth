# Phase III scientific status

This publication copy reports historical September 2026 research; publication preparation did not retrain or sample new data. Authorship and paths were prepared for public release. Source proofs and reported conclusions remain research claims rather than external peer-review certification.

Theoretical support includes Brier gain/movement/variance identities, an explicit worst-case paired-testing cost proportional to S/Delta^2, and a supplied signed ReLU-module sequence comparing fresh paired-loss-only audits with precommitted shared evidence. The lower bound excludes off-action observations and assumes conditional power; it is not a lower bound for arbitrary supervised neural learning. Fresh EB and fixed-mixture betting gates have conditional sequence guarantees on deployed checkpoints. Warmup, diagnostic tails, heuristic mean gates and unbounded-loss complementarity experiments are outside those guarantees.

The original confirmation contains 192 task/seed/protocol configurations, 832 trajectories and 6,656 opportunities. The separately frozen betting/EB follow-up adds 128 configurations, 256 trajectories and 2,048 opportunities: 1,088 trajectories and 8,704 opportunities in total. A 64-run matched-width full-label-pool diagnostic is explicitly post hoc. The auxiliary module and complementary-feature studies are not ordinary hidden-parameter training.

Repeated growth works on some stationary nonlinear tasks and valid gates retain small null/linear models. Residual proposals do not consistently beat random; on the larger-audit interaction task residual minus random Brier risk is +0.003168 with descriptive 95% half-width 0.002128. Betting improves residual risk relative to EB by about -0.00861 (additive) and -0.02349 (interaction) at the same audit budget, while raising subsequent training work about 20.6% and 22.4%. Offline same-final-width full-label-pool controls have lower mean risk in every task, with optimizer, label-timing and retrospective-width caveats.

The original unsigned constant-target module witness was corrected because an intercept could solve its target. Its code, theorem draft and numerical summary remain under `research/unsigned_diagnostic/`, explicitly superseded by the signed formulation. The main lower bound does not explain every EB stall: the sufficient-bound linear floor causes much observed conservatism. General reusable evidence after changing learned proposals, semantic specialization, universal entropy/CMI rules, real-data/deep-network benefits and globally optimal stopping remain open.
