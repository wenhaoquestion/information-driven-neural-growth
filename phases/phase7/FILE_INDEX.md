# Phase 7 file map

- `SCIENTIFIC_STATUS.md`: precise claims, negative results, historical evidence and current validation limits.
- `REPRODUCE.md`: dependencies and optional deterministic arithmetic/document commands.
- `PHASE7_DECISION_ZH.md`: scientific decision note and explicitly unexecuted future research proposals.
- `TECHNICAL_REVIEW.md`: concise history of technical corrections and internal checks.
- `SOURCE_INDEX.md`: historical source citations and content hashes.
- `manuscript/`: complete integrated English LaTeX source and bibliography.
- `output/pdf/`: archival English PDF and sanitized Chinese publication PDF.
- `research/memory/`: standalone proof/PDF, exact-arithmetic checker and saved results.
- `research/finite/`: final equal-mass proof, earlier Shannon-copy proof, two checkers, saved equal-mass results and qualified literature audit.
- `research/sketch/`: classical approximate-query section source, included in the main manuscript.
- `research/information/`: standalone classical appendix source/PDF, bibliography and research decision.
- `qa/`: document build scripts and sanitized Chinese TeX source only.

Internal collaboration records, local logs, environment/cache files, repeated page renders, installation receipts and duplicate archives are omitted. No numerical training dataset is required by the Phase 7 proofs.
