# Scientific status

This is a research working paper, not an externally peer-reviewed publication or a certification of historical priority. Curvature degree means the degree of `g = φ″`, not of the generator φ.

For four equal-mass states and capacities two and three, the paper gives deterministic and stochastic worst-case hierarchy prices of Θ(r/log r). With arbitrary positive weights and posterior gaps allowed to degenerate with r, it constructs Ω(r²/log² r) prices. Its general two-capacity upper bound is O(r²). For arbitrary finite sources and all capacities, the v2 upper factor is `256 r² [2 log(64r²) + 1/4]`; constant curvature is treated separately with factor 32. Phase 9 later proves the matching four-state upper order Θ(r²/log² r). The arbitrary-source degree order remains unsettled.

Finite enumeration and quadrature check constructed instances and implementations. Numerical stochastic values are lower/upper brackets, not certified global optima. The largest sampled endpoint construction has a large relative price but normalized OPT₃ near 4.68×10⁻²⁷. Sampled n≤8192 does not meet every coarse sufficient bound in the asymptotic proof; a conservative analytic threshold is n≥65536.

No neural training benefit, sample-generalization result, efficient growth algorithm, or nonvanishing absolute-loss advantage follows. This late theoretical work is not evidence that earlier training stages succeeded. Source access limits and classical attributions remain explicit in the paper and ledger.
