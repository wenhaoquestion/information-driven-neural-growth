# Scientific status

This is a theoretical working paper, with exact finite computational checks. It is not external peer review, formal verification, or evidence of successful neural-network training.

Established scope: last two nontrivial capacities $(N-2,N-1)$, positive source masses, distinct ordered binary posteriors, and nonnegative nonzero polynomial curvature of degree at most $r$. General prescribed capacities and simultaneous all-capacity sharp degree orders remain open. The lower-bound witnesses may depend on $N,r$; minimum masses, spacings and a common practical loss are not fixed. Normalized flat excess risks in the witnesses vanish.

The finite checks cover 300 instances, 67,860 compatible hierarchy candidates and 807 selected flat-optimum combinations. They supplement the proofs; the 807 combinations are recorded as a count rather than a complete row-by-row trace. No stochastic hierarchy optimization, far-anchor asymptotic numerical experiment, training, or generalization study was performed here.
