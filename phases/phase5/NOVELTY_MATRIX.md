## Important stronger alternatives and invalid transfers

- Li–Ma–Zhang arXiv1712.09203v5 Thm1.2 learns actual Gaussian quadratic directions with a fixed overparameterized network and a modified/truncated optimizer. Its noiseless norm/initialization assumptions do not prove our Adam result, but fixed feature learning is already possible.
- Jin et al. ICML2023/arXiv2301.11500v1 Thm4.1 covers a whole sequence of ranks under restrictive matrix RIP and small initialization. Ordinary Gaussian outer products are not ordinary isotropic Gaussian matrix measurements; cannot import that RIP.
- Growing Tiny Networks arXiv2405.19816v2 Props3.2–3.3/AppC.2 already optimizes empirical residual cross-moments via SVD in its linearized neural growth model. The current quadratic argument must be compared to this, not only to earlier project tanh experiments.
- Huang2608.02267 is v2 (2026-09-12) in the checked current record. Repair lies outside its fixed-kernel theorem; naming its open repair problem is not an original contribution.
- Sharan–Sidford–Valiant1904.08544v2 uses branching-program bit memory. Our matrix dimensions and exact real moments are not a replacement memory lower-bound model.
- Efficient streaming Gram sketches already support approximate ridge regression. Storing a lifted fourth-order input operator is ordinary normal-equation retention, not a new theorem from its tensor terminology.

## Source-access and priority risks

The primary original Blackwell proof was inaccessible in this run; no load-bearing theorem is claimed from it. Chen et al. (2026), *A unified analysis on cross-architecture generalizability of coresets*, DOI10.1016/j.patcog.2026.113730, has checked publisher/institution metadata and official code but full theorem access remained unresolved. It is a live priority risk for any future cross-architecture coreset claim. Searches and source comparisons are targeted, not exhaustive worldwide priority verification. Recent asymptotic quadratic-width theory is partly heuristic in its stated finite-size extension; it may block conceptual novelty without proving the finite-sample theorem we seek.

## Remaining genuine research obligation

No argument here proves an unknown-rank online learner simultaneously attains optimal noisy prediction/width/work under matched finite precision and storage, nor that structural changes add value over competent fixed online learning. That is an unresolved target, not a theorem rescued by new terminology. The record deliberately distinguishes exact negative diagnostics, inherited ingredients, new executed experiments, and the unfulfilled central originality goal.
