# Phase 6 — historical information under a fixed work budget

The historical 256-seed mean-AUC conjunction passed (IUT p=0.00311735). H-minus-random128 and H-minus-random138 means were −0.00058145 and −0.00043108. This is a narrow Gaussian quadratic study of complete algorithms, with scheduled growth from one to seven units. It does not establish a new general growth principle or autonomous architecture selection.

The fixed-width contrast is tail-sensitive: H wins on only 118/256 paired seeds. Random growth is better at the smallest evaluated budget; the threshold endpoint does not demonstrate minimum-cost savings; fixed width is faster in the recorded learner timings. Secondary tasks do not show uniform H superiority. These qualifications and all adverse outcomes are retained.

Start with the [English working paper](output/pdf/phase6_working_paper.pdf), [Chinese decision note](PHASE6_DECISION_ZH.md), [scientific status](SCIENTIFIC_STATUS.md), [file index](FILE_INDEX.md), and [reproduction instructions](REPRODUCE.md). `manuscript/main.tex` contains the full paper and complete scoped proofs.

The public record includes all 10 configurations and seeds, frozen numerical source, original prospective identity records, 7,040 confirmation/sensitivity seed-level trajectory rows, complete per-category work rows, every development score, all target/nonattainer and optimizer-age tables, figures, and historical audit results. The 960 development trajectories and two 32-trajectory smoke runs are identified by their completion records and summary evidence. Large generated arrays and per-event parameter histories remain local. [EXCLUDED_ARTIFACTS.csv](EXCLUDED_ARTIFACTS.csv) lists every exclusion with its original relative path, size, SHA-256 and reason; there is no public raw-data download in this repository.

Historical audits are internal technical checks, not external peer review or proof of personal author review. The publication pass did not train, simulate, draw random samples, run bootstrap, or repeat the scientific study.
