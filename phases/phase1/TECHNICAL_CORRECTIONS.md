# Historical technical corrections and boundaries

The preliminary KL/Hellinger inequality initially bounded the likelihood ratio only on the support of Q. That does not imply absolute continuity: P=(1/2,1/2), Q=(1,0) has an infinite KL divergence. The final proof instead assumes P_j <= M Q_j for every coordinate. Actual mixture centroids already satisfy this condition, so this correction changes the auxiliary hypothesis, not the source-mass theorem or its constant.

The numerical record was expanded to retain 60-digit stochastic risks/bounds and total hierarchy coordinates. Four 70-extra-digit comparisons cover six principal scalars. This prevents a stored binary64 construction risk from supporting an overstated high-precision claim. The final saved files contain the expanded fields.

The general-generator theorem is only an excess-risk statement. Its rare mass may need to be reduced below epsilon squared to justify the fixed-epsilon differentiability remainder. It does not assume uniform boundary curvature. The stochastic feasible construction matches the asymptotic lower bound but is not asserted to optimize every finite L.

The independent onset draft is preserved as proposed secondary work. Its strict KL/local-coefficient gap is essential; its multiple-maximizer and cardinality analysis still needs broader novelty/correctness scrutiny. It was not incorporated into the main paper.

This report restates technical issues from historical project checks. It does not reproduce internal review dialogues or certify independent external peer review.
