# Publication-copy scope

The supplied author name and affiliation were inserted into previously empty manuscript author fields. The manuscript PDF is built from this public source. Scientific statements, equations, reported numbers, negative findings and limitations were preserved. Portable paths and a saved-evidence-only reproduction entrypoint were added; internal coordination/persona text was removed from technical notes. These edits are publication preparation, not a new study, author approval of every claim or external peer review.

Original scientific source hashes, transformed artifact hashes and the exact selection map are retained in a private preparation record. Public raw manifests identify retained local evidence; compressed evidence manifests identify the exact bytes supplied. Third-party full-text papers are not redistributed. Bibliographic citations and historical source locations remain available.
