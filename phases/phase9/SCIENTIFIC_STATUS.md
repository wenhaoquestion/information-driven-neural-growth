# Scientific status

This is a research working paper, not external peer review, formal verification, or historical-priority certification. Curvature degree r refers to φ″.

For exactly four states, arbitrary positive source weights and capacities two and three, the paper gives matching deterministic and stochastic worst-case orders Θ(r²/log² r), with an explicit upper factor 1936r²/log²r for r≥2. Capacities one and four are free extensions in this four-state setting. The theorem does not settle arbitrary source counts; the broader O(r²) two-capacity and O(r² log(r+2)) all-capacity upper bounds remain the applicable known project bounds.

A single three-state source can force Θ(r² log r) distortion in the global h=∫√g comparison method while its actual hierarchy price is exactly one. Thus comparison distortion is not a hierarchy-price lower bound. The finite indicator-ReLU equivalence requires known finite populations, restricted hidden indicator weights, arbitrary subset splits and exact population refits. An unrestricted unit on one-hot inputs can encode all posteriors, so the restricted lower bound does not transfer to general network width.

Saved exact and floating-point calculations check formulas and implementations, not neural training. Bounded-loss normalization still permits flat excess costs to vanish. No finite-sample generalization, SGD/Adam guarantee, compute saving or nonvanishing absolute-risk advantage follows. These late papers are not proof of earlier training success.
