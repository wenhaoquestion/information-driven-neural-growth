## Finite representation line

For every four-state source with masses at least gamma and any finite target alphabet, the manuscript proves a deterministic two/three-state hierarchy with simultaneous excess and total Bayes log-loss factor at most `2 + 2 sqrt(3 + log(1/gamma))`, including zero-optimum cases without division. The rare-state family supplies the matching worst-case order `Theta(sqrt(log(1/gamma)))` for deterministic and stochastic Markov hierarchies. Sharp family constants do not identify the optimal universal leading constant. These are population results about finite encoding states; no neuron-count, optimization, or practical sample-efficiency conclusion follows. Absolute losses in the rare-state family vanish.

Historical computations include exhaustive enumeration of 15 partitions and 18 compatible chains, 15 scale cases, 10 sensitivity cases, 4,000 entropy-vector diagnostics, 1,204 distribution instances, and 14 higher-precision checks. The same sampled family has squared-loss hierarchy factor one. Computational agreement is not a replacement for proof, and worldwide priority is not certified.
