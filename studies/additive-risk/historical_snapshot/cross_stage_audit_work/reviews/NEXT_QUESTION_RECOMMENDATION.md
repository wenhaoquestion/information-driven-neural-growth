# Historical study scope

The next question was whether the ten existing witnesses have detectable additive hierarchy conflict when the full-domain outcome-wise loss oscillation is exactly one. The proposed thresholds were Delta >= 0.001, null Delta <= 0.0005, at most one million labels for a preselected candidate, 5% size and 80% pointwise power. Known masses, public losses and unknown posterior were essential. This recommendation preceded the additive study; it did not itself establish those conclusions.
