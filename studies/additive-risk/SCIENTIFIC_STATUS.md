# Scientific status

The population outcome is positive for three interior candidates and negative at the 0.001 threshold for seven endpoint candidates. The single labeled candidate is interior $n=2048$; $n=4096$ and $8192$ have population certificates only.

The estimand is population-optimal hierarchy regret for four observable states, known masses $p_i=1/4$, a custom fixed loss and independent identically distributed Bernoulli labels. It is not a trained model's random risk, accuracy gain, identity of an optimal partition, or benefit of growing neurons. Losses differ across candidate degrees. Coverage is uniform over all posteriors with that same public loss and masses; the 80% power bound is only at the specified reference posterior.

The oracle is valid on the full report domain and returns conservative intervals outside its narrow tail region. Inference consumes only the public count schema and loss parameters. Simulation and inference ran in the same process: this is a reviewed data dependency, not external blinding or process isolation.

Local locks, source hashes and timestamps support one recorded confirmation attempt. They cannot certify the absence of unrecorded external seed trials. The fixed pseudorandom realization is not an empirical coverage or power study. Unknown masses, ordinary losses, learned representations, training, generalization and resource savings remain unestablished.
