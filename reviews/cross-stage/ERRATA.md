An earlier Phase 8 explanatory sentence described normalization to a bounded strictly proper loss as fixing the loss scale. The correct statement is that it gives a common **upper bound**, not a nonzero fixed amplitude. Thus boundedness and a large hierarchy price cannot alone establish a large absolute-risk effect.

This correction does not withdraw curvature-degree, hierarchy-price, stochastic-comparison or normalization-legality theorems.
